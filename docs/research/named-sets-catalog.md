Best sources (5 lines):
1. Coach's Clipboard (coachesclipboard.net, Dr. James Gels): the largest named catalog in one place, with hundreds of man sets, 2-3 and 1-3-1 zone plays, BLOB/SLOB families (Box, Stack, 4-Low, Wheel, Tap Head) and buzzer beaters; free pages give sequences, premium adds diagrams.
2. FastModel PlayBank (playbank.fastmodelsports.com): 1,400+ BLOBs, 1,200+ Horns, searchable by team and action (Rip STS, Spain, Zoom, Veer); best for real team attributions (Heat, Pacers, Mercury, Fenerbahce, Florida, Alabama, Lakers, Real Madrid) and modern NBA naming.
3. DNVR Nuggets playbook guide plus the Basketball Action Dictionary (Medium) and Pere's substack glossary: the cleanest definitions of NBA terminology (Pistol/21, Drag, 77, Delay series, Elbow series, Dive, Veer, Ghost, Step up, Empty side, Gator, Miami, Flip).
4. Coaching Toolbox (coachingtoolbox.net) and Hoops Geek (thehoopsgeek.com): concrete step-by-step SLOBs and BLOBs from pro, WNBA and college teams (Stevens Winner, Brush Clear, MSU, Xavier, Sparks, Point Loma Two series, Iowa, Davidson, Pittsburgh).
5. Hooper University press breakers and coachprincetonbasketball.com: the only sources that gave press breaks and Princeton (Chin, Point Over/Away/Under, Low, Twirl, X) as explicit numbered sequences with reads; Breakthrough Basketball and Hoops King round out 1-4 across and diamond-press attacks.
