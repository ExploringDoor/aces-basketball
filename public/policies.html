Policies

Last updated: November 2026

The Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy cover the whole aceshoops.com site. The refund policy and registration terms apply to registrations for Lower Merion Aces Basketball (“Aces”) clinics. Registration payments are collected by Always Competing Sports LLC on behalf of the clinic’s organizing booster club and remitted to them.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clinic spots are limited, and staffing and gym time are committed in advance. Because of this:

  • Registration fees are non-refundable except as described below.
  • If your player is injured or must withdraw, email us at aceshoops@gmail.com and we’ll issue a credit toward a future Aces clinic.
  • If Aces cancels a session (weather, gym closure), we email every registered family as soon as the call is made, and you’ll receive a credit or refund for that session.
  • The processing fee portion of each registration is non-refundable.

To request a credit or ask about a cancellation, email aceshoops@gmail.com with your receipt number.

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy covers the whole aceshoops.com site.

What we collect at clinic registration

When you register a player, we collect: the player’s name, date of birth, and grade; the parent/guardian’s name, email, and phone; an emergency contact; an optional authorized-pickup contact; optional health information you choose to share (allergies, medications, medical conditions); and your typed waiver signature with the date and IP address at the time of signing.

Why we collect it

To run the clinic safely, communicate with you about the program, process your payment, and keep accurate registration records.

Payments

Card payments are processed securely by our payment processor (Stripe). We never see or store your full card number.

Other forms on the site

  • Aces Newsletter: your email and, if you add it, your name, so we can send the newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Schedule change alerts: your email, the teams you follow, and whether you are a player, parent or coach, so we can email you when a game or practice moves. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Shoot-a-Thon pledges: your name, email, an optional message, and your IP address (to stop spam and duplicate pledges). The public supporter wall shows only your first name and last initial and your message, or “Anonymous” if you choose that.
  • Team store orders: Stripe collects your email and phone at checkout, and we keep them with the order so we can reach you about it.
  • Contact form and alumni check-in: what you type is emailed to the Aces at aceshoops@gmail.com so we can reply. It is not used for anything else.

How it’s stored and who sees it

Registration information is stored in a secured database and is accessible only to Aces administrators and coaching staff who need it to run the clinic. We do not sell your information. We share it only with our payment processor (to take payment) and with the coaching staff (to run the clinic and respond to emergencies). Payments are collected by Always Competing Sports LLC on behalf of the booster club, as noted above.

Analytics & cookies

To see how the site is used, we use two analytics tools: Vercel Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, and Google Analytics, which uses cookies. They tell us which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are (city or region, never an exact location), which outside site sent them here, and what kind of device and browser they use.

What these tools receive about a visit: the address of the page, with anything after a “?” or “#” removed (so receipt numbers, codes and unsubscribe links stay private); the outside site you came from, such as a search engine; and basic device information. They never receive what you type into a form, your name, email or phone, or player information. Coach and admin tools on the site are not measured at all. Google signals and ad personalization are turned off, and we never use analytics for advertising. We don’t use your registration data for advertising, and we don’t sell it.

Google’s own explanation of what it does with this information is at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can block Google Analytics on every website with Google’s free opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout, or by blocking cookies in your browser. The site works the same either way. The cookies themselves are listed in the Cookie Policy below.

Keeping & deleting your information

We keep registration records only as long as needed to run the program and for our records. To ask what we have on file or to request deletion, email aceshoops@gmail.com.

Children’s information

We only collect a child’s information from the registering parent or guardian, and only what’s needed to run the clinic safely.

Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Here is every cookie aceshoops.com uses, and the other places information is kept in your browser.

Cookies we set

  • Google Analytics (_ga and _ga_3BSYNRJHXZ): tell one visit from another so we can count visitors and see which pages are read. They last up to two years. They hold a random number, not your name or any contact details.

That is the only cookie we set. Vercel Web Analytics, our other counter, uses no cookies at all. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not let advertisers place cookies on the site.

Other things saved in your browser

  • Clinic registration: the form keeps what you have typed in that browser tab, so a refresh doesn’t erase it. It is deleted when you close the tab or finish registering.
  • Coach and admin tools: remember a coach’s sign-in on that device so they don’t have to type it every time.

Cookies from other companies

  • Payments happen on Stripe’s secure checkout page, which uses its own cookies to keep payments safe and stop fraud. See Stripe’s cookie settings.
  • Instagram posts on the Follow Us page are shown straight from Instagram, which may set its own cookies when that page is open. See Instagram’s cookie policy.

Your choices

You can delete or block cookies in your browser’s settings at any time, and the site keeps working. To stop Google Analytics on every site you visit, use Google’s opt-out add-on. Questions? Email aceshoops@gmail.com.
