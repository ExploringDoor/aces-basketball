How codes work

Send each player their own code. Text it, don't post it in a group chat.
  1. Players go to /playbook.html and enter their code.
  2. They stay signed in on that phone for 30 days.
  3. Turn a player off and they're locked out instantly. A new code logs them out everywhere.
  4. Use groups (Varsity, JV) to show different plays to different teams.

Teams

One program, several teams. Each team has its own roster, plans, practices, box scores and Attitude Board; plays and the glossary are shared unless you limit a play to a team. Pick a team at the top of the board to work on just that one.
Players use the same team name as their group. A play limited to a team only shows to that team.

Assistant code

A second passcode for assistants and managers. They can run the Board, log bench calls, keep box scores, write player notes, make game plans and practices, and set tonight's plays. They cannot draw or edit plays, change players, or touch settings.
Assistants sign in on this same page with their code. Change it any time; the old one stops working right away.

Sunday night email

Every Sunday around 8pm you get one email: who studied this week and who didn't, quiz average, what's due with who still owes it, questions waiting, box scores added, bench calls, and the week's calendar.
Goes to the coach email on file for the site.

What players can and can't do

  • Their name and the time are stamped across every play, so a photo is traceable to them.
  • The page hides itself the moment they switch apps, and after 10 minutes idle.
  • Printing, saving, copying and right click are turned off.
  • Every play they open is logged under Who's studying.
  • A phone can still photograph a screen. The stamp is what makes that a bad idea.
